FAQ

Common questions

How much does Microsoft 365 setup cost in the UAE?
Microsoft 365 setup starts from AED 500 for up to 5 users and AED 1,200 for up to 25 users. The setup fee is separate from Microsoft's monthly licence cost. We provide a fixed quote after a free consultation — no hourly billing.
How long does Microsoft 365 setup take?
A typical setup takes 1–2 business days. Email migration timing depends on how much data needs to be migrated. Your team can continue using their current email throughout the process — we switch everything over at the end.
Can you migrate our email from Gmail or another provider to Microsoft 365?
Yes. We migrate email from any platform — Gmail, cPanel hosting, Yahoo, another Microsoft tenant, or any other provider — to Microsoft 365 with zero data loss. All emails, contacts and calendar entries are migrated.
