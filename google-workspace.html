How much does Google Workspace setup cost in UAE?
Google Workspace setup starts from AED 500 for up to 5 users. Google's monthly licence fee is separate and billed directly by Google. We provide a fixed quote after a free consultation — no hourly billing.
How long does Google Workspace migration take?
Most setups and migrations are complete within 1–2 business days. Your team can continue using their current email throughout — we migrate everything and switch DNS at the end to minimise disruption.

Yes. We handle complete migrations from Microsoft 365, cPanel, Yahoo, or any other email platform to Google Workspace. All emails, contacts and calendar data are migrated with zero data loss.
