FAQ

Questions businesses ask us

Straight answers. No sales talk.

Do you provide Microsoft 365 setup for businesses in Dubai and Sharjah?
Yes. We set up Microsoft 365 for businesses across Dubai, Sharjah and Abu Dhabi. This includes creating your tenant, setting up user accounts, configuring Teams, SharePoint, OneDrive, and Exchange Online. We also handle email migration from your current provider.
Can you migrate our existing email to Google Workspace or Microsoft 365?
Yes. We handle complete email migrations from any platform — cPanel hosting, Outlook, Gmail, or any other provider — to either Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace. We migrate all your emails, contacts and calendar data with zero downtime for your team.
How much does Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace setup cost?
Our services start from AED 500 for setup (up to 5 users) and AED 1,200 for up to 25 users. We offer a free consultation to assess your requirements first, then provide a fixed quote before any work begins. No surprises, no hourly billing traps.
Do you offer ongoing IT support for small businesses?
Yes. We offer ongoing IT support packages for small and medium businesses. This covers Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace management, helpdesk support for your team, user onboarding and offboarding, and general IT queries. Available remotely across the UAE.
Can you work remotely or do you need to visit our office?
Most of our work is done remotely — Microsoft 365 setup, Google Workspace migration, email configuration and IT support can all be handled without visiting your office. For specific hardware or network setup tasks we can visit your location in Dubai, Sharjah or Abu Dhabi.
How quickly do you respond to enquiries?
We target a 30-minute response on WhatsApp and email, 7 days a week. For urgent IT issues we aim to start working on your problem the same day. You'll always be speaking directly with our technical team — no call centres, no ticket queues.
