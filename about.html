Our Story

Built for Businesses That Need IT to Just Work

CoreFix Solutions started from a simple observation: too many businesses in the UAE were struggling with avoidable IT problems — misconfigured email accounts, failed migrations, unsecured cloud setups — because they couldn't access affordable, reliable IT expertise.

We set out to fix that. Today we help small and medium businesses across Dubai, Sharjah and Abu Dhabi get their Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, and IT infrastructure set up correctly from day one — and keep it running smoothly after.

No bloated retainers. No confusing jargon. No waiting days for a response. Just clear advice, fast execution, and IT support that actually answers the phone.
